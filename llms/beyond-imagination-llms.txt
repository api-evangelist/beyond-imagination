## Products

- [AURA](https://www.beyondimagination.com/products/): Adaptive Unified Robotic/Human Administrator — a universal operating system for coordinating robots, humans and infrastructure across manufacturing ecosystems.
- [ARCANA](https://www.beyondimagination.com/ai/): An LLM-free synthetic brain architecture modeled on the human mind, powering real-time perception, decision making and coordination.
- [Expert Minds](https://www.beyondimagination.com/products/): Modular AI job modules trained for specialized roles — chemical handling, bio-reactor operation, surgical prep, warehouse logistics.
- [Hive Mind Intelligence](https://www.beyondimagination.com/products/): Fleet learning — robots upload new skills from live operations and video into a shared cloud memory.
- Beomni: A wheeled humanoid robot with an articulated upper body and dexterous hands, operable via VR headset and haptic gloves or autonomously.
